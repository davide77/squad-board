"likely" means seen in search results or the page loaded but could not be tied to the organisation for certain. Follower counts are as loaded on 1 October 2026.

Most of these run on Facebook and FA Full-Time instead.
- **Surrey Youth League:** an account @surreyyouthleague exists (95 followers, 3 posts) but nothing ties it to the league. Ask Frank Thompson before following.
- **@tyfl:** exists (224 followers, 1,194 posts) with no name or bio we could read. Could be Tandridge. Check by eye before following.
- **@scwgfl** is the Sussex league, not Surrey (the Surrey league uses SCWGL).
- **Soccer Coach Weekly:** the website links X, Facebook and YouTube but no Instagram. @soccer_coach_weekly has the right name and only 4 posts, so it may not be theirs.
- **FA Coachcast:** no separate account, it lives on @englandfootballlearning.
- **The Soccer Coaching Podcast:** X only (@SoccerCoachCast), no Instagram found.
- **Grassroots Football Week:** no UK account found. The September week is UEFA Grassroots Week, run through the national FAs. The Carling grassroots campaign was left out (alcohol).
- **FA Respect and FA girls' football:** no separate Instagram accounts. Both are covered by @englandfootball.
- **Scottish FA:** its website links only the national team (@scotlandnationalteam). @scottishfa is nearly empty. No Scottish youth FA account found.
- **FAI:** fai.ie links no Instagram. @faireland looks official but is unconfirmed.
- **Left out on purpose:** club and academy pages that mostly post children (for example Girls United FA, We Make Footballers), betting, alcohol and junk food brands, and big US or global coaching influencers.
