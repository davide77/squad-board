# Instagram playbook - @gafferboard

The plan for Gafferboard on Instagram: the story we tell, who we tell it to, how often, in what voice, and how we know it is working. Read this before drafting any post. [brand.md](../../brand.md) still wins on voice, colour and type.

Account: **@gafferboard**, opened 1 October 2026.

---

## 1. Why Instagram, and what it is for

Gafferboard is a free tool that spreads coach to coach. Instagram has three jobs in that:

1. **Introduce it.** A coach who hears about Gafferboard from a league email, a parent or a team sheet will look it up. The profile has to answer "what is it, who made it, is it any good" in ten seconds.
2. **Be there on Friday night.** That is when coaches pick the team. A useful post in the feed that evening is a reminder that there is a better way than the magnet board.
3. **Make it credible.** Journalists, County FAs and future sponsors check the socials before they reply. A tidy, active account backs up the outreach already sent (see the outreach notes in memory).

What Instagram is **not** for: selling, ads near kids, or chasing follower numbers. The number that matters for the project is still **team sheets sent per week**. Instagram is judged on whether it sends coaches to the board.

### How it fits the project

```
League emails, County FAs, press  ─┐
Instagram (story, how-to, Friday) ─┼─> gafferboard.com ─> board ─> team sheet to parents
Word of mouth                     ─┘                                  │
           ^                                                          │
           └──────── "Made with gafferboard.com" on the sheet <───────┘
```

The team sheet is the loop. Instagram feeds the top of it.

## 2. The background story

This is the story every launch post tells, and the one the bio and pinned posts keep telling to anyone who arrives later.

**In one paragraph.** Davide builds websites for a living and has coached grassroots football since his son was in the under 9s. He's still on the touchline with the under 15s. Every Saturday was the same: the squad in a WhatsApp group, the shape on a magnet board, the subs on the back of a teamsheet and the minutes in his head. Then it rains and the magnets slide. So he built the board he wanted: squad, who's in, the shape, the bench and every sub to the minute, on the phone already in his pocket, with the team sheet going to the parents in the coach's own name. It knows the age group, so under 11s get equal minutes and from under 12s it's about results. It's free for your team. No account, nothing to install.

**The beats, in order** (the story carousel follows them):

1. The moment: Saturday morning, more kids than shirts.
2. The coach: Davide, a web developer and a parent who coaches.
3. The mess: WhatsApp, magnets, the back of a teamsheet, the head.
4. The weather: it rains, the kit fails. The weather and the tech are the only fair jokes.
5. The fix: the board he wanted.
6. The detail that proves he coaches: age group sets the format and fair minutes.
7. The parents: who's in goes out in the coach's name. Never the shape, never who's injured.
8. The offer: free for your team, no account.

**Facts to keep straight.**

- Do not print a number of years or seasons. "Since my son was in the under 9s" is true and safe.
- Never name his son, any player, or a child at the club.
- Ayat is in the background. If it comes up: "I coach an under 15s side. Gafferboard is my own project, not the club's."
- Do not claim "nothing else like it". Say it plainly: other apps run the club, Gafferboard is for the 90 minutes on the touchline.

## 4. Voice on Instagram

Two speakers, never mixed in one post.

**The Gaffer (most posts).** The account speaks Hairdryer, like the website: short, flat, dry, on your side. "Four taps. Every Saturday." "Who's in? Tick them." Marketing and social posts are where brand.md says to "turn it up", so the best lines go here.

**Davide (the maker's notes, about one post in six).** First person, plain, practical, signed "- Davide". Contractions, short paragraphs, `really` as his intensifier, no polish. He asks coaches what's missing and means it. Read `~/.claude/email-voice.md` for how he actually writes.

**Pat on the back** shows up only in posts about the youngest age groups (under 7s to under 11s), and then for the whole post, because that's the voice those coaches' boards use. Rule 11 of brand.md: one way per line.

All of brand.md's hard rules apply, plus these for Instagram:

- **No long dashes**, anywhere, ever. The server refuses a caption that has one.
- **Five hashtags at most.** Instagram caps posts at five. Use them at the end, from the bank below.
- **First line carries the post.** Only about 125 characters show before "more". Put the hook there.
- **One call to action per post.** "Link in bio", "Save this for Friday", or "Tell me what's missing". Never all three.
- **No engagement bait.** No "tag a coach who...", no "comment YES". Ask real questions only.
- **Emojis:** none in the Gaffer's posts. Davide may use one where he would in a message.

**Hashtag bank.** Core: `#grassrootsfootball #youthfootball #footballcoach #juniorfootball #grassrootscoach`. Rotate in: `#minisoccer` `#u9football` `#u11football` `#u12football` `#girlsfootball` `#sundayleague` `#footballmanager` `#coachinglife` `#ukfootball`. Pick the five that fit the post.

## 7. The weekly rhythm

Built around the grassroots week. Youth football is mostly Saturday or Sunday morning, training midweek, team picked Thursday to Friday.

| Day | Feed | Stories |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | **Fair go** or **Touchline** carousel, 18:30 | "How did it go?" one frame |
| Tue | - | Training night, one tip frame |
| Wed | - | Maker's note or a feature frame |
| Thu | **Matchday** how-to (carousel or Reel), 19:00 | "Team picked yet?" |
| Fri | **Reel** when we have one, otherwise a short carousel, 19:30 | Two or three frames: pick the team tonight |
| Sat | - | 07:30 "Matchday. Who's in?" |
| Sun | Maker's note every other week, 18:00 | 07:30 matchday frame for Sunday leagues |

That is **three feed posts and daily stories**. Fewer, better posts beat a daily grid that runs out of things to say. If a week is busy, the Thursday post is the one that must go out.

**Reels.** Small accounts grow through Reels and shares. The cheapest good Reel is a phone screen recording of the demo board (gafferboard.com, "Have a go"): 10 to 20 seconds, one action, text on the first frame. One a week from week 3.

## 8. Visual system

- Every slide is made by [tools/social/render.mjs](../../tools/social/render.mjs) from a JSON spec in [posts/](posts/). Board background, Chalk text, one kit-yellow accent, Saira Condensed headlines, Barlow body, the yellow top rule from the board, the logo in the footer.
- Sizes: feed **1080x1350** (4:5), stories **1080x1920** (9:16). JPEG only, which the renderer makes.
- Layouts: `cover`, `text`, `list`, `screen` (a board screenshot in a phone frame), `end`.
- Put `*one word*` in asterisks to set it in yellow. Once per slide at most.
- Screenshots come from the real board or the demo team (the made-up players). Never a real squad.
- The film and the onboarding clips are AI-made. Post the film from the Instagram app with the **AI label** switched on, and never let the AI coach stand in for Davide.

## 9. Red lines

Children play this game. These are not up for debate:

- **No photos or video of real children.** Not from matches, not from training, not blurred, not from behind. This includes Davide's own team.
- **No real player names**, no real squad lists, no real team sheets. Use the demo team.
- **No other club's badge or name** without their written OK.
- **No betting, alcohol or junk food** anywhere near the account, including sponsors and collabs.
- **No mocking anyone:** players, refs, parents, other teams or other apps. Weather and tech only.
- **Nothing published without Davide's OK on that exact post.** Claude drafts, Davide approves, then it goes out.

## 12. How we grow it

What works for a small, useful account in 2026, in order of effort to reward:

1. **Shares and saves over likes.** Instagram ranks posts that get sent in DMs and saved. Make posts worth forwarding to the assistant coach: a how-to, a format table, a fair minutes plan.
2. **Reels from week 3.** Screen recordings of the board. Short, one action, captioned.
3. **Collab posts.** Invite a grassroots coaching account or a County FA to a joint post on something useful to their followers (formats, fair minutes). The post shows on both profiles.
4. **Davide's replies.** Fifteen minutes a day on other coaches' posts, as a coach, not as an advert.
5. **The team sheet loop.** Every sheet sent carries the name. Instagram should mention "it's on the bottom of the sheet" now and then, so parents who see it know where it comes from.
6. **No paid promotion** until organic posts show which messages work. Then a small boost on the best Friday post, in August, aimed at coaches. Never at parents of young children.

## 13. Measuring it

**Every Monday** (Claude runs this from `ig_recent_media` and `ig_insights`, then writes it into [log.md](log.md)):

- Per post: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link taps.
- Best and worst post of the week and why.
- Visits to gafferboard.com from Instagram (Vercel Analytics, referrer `instagram.com` and `l.instagram.com`).
- Team sheets sent that week (the project's one number), to see whether Instagram moves it.

**Targets, honest ones.**

| By | Followers | What matters more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 31 October 2026 | 100 to 200, mostly coaches | One post with 20 or more shares. Instagram visible as a referrer in analytics. |
| 31 December 2026 | 500 | A collab with a County FA or coaching account. Saves above likes on how-to posts. |
| 31 August 2027 | 2,000 | Instagram is a top three source of new boards in the new season. |

If after eight weeks Instagram sends almost nobody to the board, cut it to the Thursday post and stories and put the time into County FAs and press instead.

## 14. Working with Claude, daily

Run `/instagram-daily` in this repo once a day, in the morning. It:

1. Reads this playbook and the log, checks the account and yesterday's numbers.
2. Works out today's slot from the weekly rhythm and the queue.
3. Drafts the post or the stories: a spec in [posts/](posts/), rendered images, caption and alt text. Shows them to Davide.
4. On his OK: commits and pushes the images (Vercel deploys them to gafferboard.com/social/...), waits until they are live, publishes through the Instagram server, and logs the permalink.
5. On Mondays, writes the weekly review. Every week, refreshes the token.

Setup of the Instagram connection: [instagram-setup.md](instagram-setup.md).

The API can post photos, carousels, Reels and stories. It cannot add story stickers (links, polls, questions), music, collab invites, or the AI label. Those posts get drafted by Claude and finished by Davide in the app.
