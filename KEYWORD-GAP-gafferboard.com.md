## 4. Pages to improve and create, in priority order

### Improve the three format pages (clusters 1, 4, 5)

Each is about 87 words today (see GEO-ANALYSIS.md). The pages that win these queries are long, shape-by-shape guides:

- Bar to beat for 7-a-side: renderfoot.com/blog/7v7-soccer-formations and footballdna.co.uk/features/formation-analysis7-a-side.
- Bar to beat for 9-a-side: soccercoachweekly.net/formats/the-ultimate-9-a-side-formation-guide.
- 11-a-side: nobody answers the youth angle well. The results are pro-football explainers and spam, so "11-a-side for a first season at under 14s" is open.

What is missing compared with them:

1. **A section per shape** (2-3-1, 3-2-1, 2-2-2, 3-1-2 for 7-a-side; 3-3-2, 3-2-3, 2-4-2, 4-3-1 for 9-a-side), each with a pitch diagram, who it suits and its weak spot. The board already has these shapes in `src/constants/football.ts`, so the diagrams can come from the same data.
2. **Age-group headings** that match what coaches type: "Best formation for under 10s", "Under 11s 7v7". These pages currently state the age once in the intro.
3. **Moving-up sections**: "From 5v5 to 7v7", "From 7v7 to 9v9". Coaches search for this and the current answers are reposted spam.
4. **Up-to-date ages.** Most competitor pages still give the pre-2026 ages (U9 at 7v7, U11 at 9v9). Gafferboard's ages are already the 2026-27 ones. Say so on the page, with the date. It is a real edge.
5. `HowTo` or `ItemList` schema for the shapes, beside the FAQ schema already there.

### Create /5-a-side (cluster 2)

The board supports 5v5 and no page targets it. From 2026-27, 5v5 is the format for under 8s and under 9s, so this is the format most new coaches start on. Same template as the improved format pages. Bar to beat: footballdna.co.uk/features/formation-analysis5-a-side and soccercoachweekly.net/formats?Topics=5v5.

### Create a fair minutes and subs page (clusters 3 and 12)

Working title: "Fair minutes for every player: a sub planner for grassroots football". This is the board's strongest feature against a SERP of App Store listings and one web tool (footballgpt.co/features/sub-planner). A web page with a working demo can beat app listings for searchers who do not want to install anything. Add an FAQ on rolling subs and sub numbers: the results for those were 2014-2018 sources and US league PDFs, so a current, plain UK answer is easy to win.

### Create a FutureFit formats page (clusters 8 and 9)

Working title: "Which format does my age group play? FA formats for 2026-27". The SERP is conflicting: the search results gave three different answers for under 9s and under 11s. One clear, dated table (U7 3v3 to U14 11v11, with pitch sizes and a link to the FA's FutureFit pages as the source) answers it. It also links naturally to every format page. Fold 3v3 under 7s in here rather than giving it its own page: there are no positions at 3v3, so a formations page would be thin.

Time-sensitive: the changes started this season, so search interest is at its peak now.

### Create a team sheet page (cluster 6)

Working title: "Send the team sheet to the parents". The "team sheet template" SERP is almost all spam pages and Word templates, and nobody answers "send the line-up to parents on WhatsApp" directly. The call-up is a Gafferboard feature nobody else in the sample offers as a web page. Show a real example of the message. This also feeds the selection-fairness topic (cluster 10) later.

### Improve the home page (clusters 7 and 11)

renderfoot.com and icoachfootball.net win "free lineup builder" with a title that says exactly that. The home title is "line-ups and team sheets for grassroots football". Consider working "free" and "no account" into the title or H1 area, since every rival listing leads with them. Do not chase "football lineup builder" as an image-maker term: Gafferboard is a matchday board, not a graphic generator, and the searcher who wants a PNG for social media will bounce.

### Ignore

- **Team management apps (15):** Spond, Pitchero and the FA's own Matchday app own it, and Gafferboard does not do payments or availability.
- **Matchday tips (14):** the FA's learning pages hold every top spot.
- **Tactics board (13):** a different job (drawing runs for training). Revisit only if the board grows that feature.

- The search tool is US-located. UK results will differ, especially for "soccer" variants and App Store listings.
- No volume or keyword difficulty data. The scores rank opportunity on fit and competition, not demand. Run the top clusters through Ahrefs or DataForSEO, or check Google Search Console impressions, before committing to an order.
